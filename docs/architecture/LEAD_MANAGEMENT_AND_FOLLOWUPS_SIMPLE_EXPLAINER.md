# Lead Management & Follow-ups — Simple Guide with Real-World Example

---

## 1. The Basics in Simple Words

* **Lead Management:** Tracks the **big sales deal** (the customer, the deal amount, and whether we win or lose the contract).
* **Follow-ups:** Tracks the **daily promises and action tasks** (the phone calls, sending price quotes, or visiting the clinic on an exact date and time).

---

## 2. A Real-World Story: How They Work Together

Let’s look at a simple story with our sales representative, **Ravi**, and a doctor, **Dr. Sharma**.

```
[ Step 1: Clinic Visit ]
Dr. Sharma wants a Holter machine
Ravi creates a LEAD (₹1,20,000)
            │
            ▼
[ Step 2: Set Follow-up ]
Doctor asks: "Send quote tomorrow by 10 AM"
Ravi attaches a FOLLOW-UP inside that Lead
            │
            ▼
[ Step 3: Morning Alert ]
App reminds Ravi under "Due Today"
Ravi sends quote and taps "Mark Done"
Doctor asks for a live demo on Friday
            │
            ▼
[ Step 4: Move the Stage ]
Ravi updates Lead stage to "QUALIFIED"
Schedules next Follow-up: "Live Demo on Friday"
            │
            ▼
[ Step 5: Close Deal (Won!) ]
Demo successful! Doctor signs contract
Ravi moves Lead to "CONVERTED" (Won)
Confirmed Revenue: ₹1,20,000
```

---

### Step 1: The Visit & Creating the Lead
* Ravi visits Dr. Sharma (Cardiologist at City Clinic).
* Dr. Sharma says: *"I want to buy a new Cardiac Holter monitor machine for my clinic."*
* Ravi opens the app and creates a **Lead**:
  * **Customer Name:** Dr. Sharma
  * **Deal Title:** Cardiac Holter Machine Purchase
  * **Estimated Value:** ₹1,20,000
  * **Stage:** `NEW`

---

### Step 2: Connecting the First Follow-up
* Dr. Sharma tells Ravi: *"Please email me the official price quotation by tomorrow morning 10:00 AM."*
* If Ravi forgets, the deal dies. So, right inside that Lead, Ravi clicks **"Add Follow-up"**:
  * **Action Type:** Quotation
  * **Date & Time:** Tomorrow at 10:00 AM
  * **Notes:** Share tier-1 clinic pricing sheet.
* **The Connection:** This task is now tied directly to Dr. Sharma’s ₹1.2 Lakh deal.

---

### Step 3: Morning Reminder & Taking Action
* The next morning, Ravi opens the app.
* Under his **"Due Today"** list, the app alerts him: *"Send Quotation to Dr. Sharma"*.
* Ravi emails the quotation.
* In the app, Ravi taps **"Mark Done"** and types what happened:  
  *"Quotation emailed. Doctor liked the price and requested a live product demo this Friday at 2 PM."*

---

### Step 4: Moving the Deal Forward (Stage Progression)
* Because the doctor is happy with the pricing, Ravi opens the Lead and moves the stage from `INTERESTED` to `QUALIFIED`.
* He immediately schedules his next follow-up: *"Live Clinic Demo — Friday 2:00 PM"*.

---

### Step 5: Closing the Deal (Won!)
* On Friday, the machine demo is successful. Dr. Sharma agrees to buy.
* Ravi moves the Lead stage to **`CONVERTED` (Won)**.
* The app asks for the final confirmation: Ravi enters the confirmed value (₹1,20,000) and writes *"Signed 1-year equipment supply agreement."*

---

## 3. In Short: How Are They Connected?

1. **The Lead is the DESTINATION:** It represents the ₹1,20,000 sales contract.
2. **The Follow-ups are the STEPS to get there:**
   * Step 1: Send Quote (Follow-up)
   * Step 2: Live Demo (Follow-up)
   * Step 3: Sign Agreement (Follow-up)

---

## 4. What If It’s Just Routine Customer Service? ("No Fake Leads")

Sometimes a doctor doesn't want to buy anything new. For example:
* Dr. Sharma says: *"Ravi, please collect my hospital renewal registration form next Tuesday."*
* This is **routine customer service**, not a new sales deal.
* In our app, Ravi can create a standalone **Follow-up** for Dr. Sharma **without creating a fake sales lead**.
* **Why this matters:** Our sales pipeline and conversion reports stay 100% accurate because routine paperwork doesn't count as sales deals.
